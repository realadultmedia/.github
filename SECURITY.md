# Security

If you find a way into member data on any of our communities or projects, email **security@realadult.media**. A person reads every report, and you'll hear back within 3 business days.

## While you look

- Don't create accounts to test. Ask, and we'll set one up for you.
- Never open, change or keep another member's data. If you reach some by accident, stop and tell us.
- No load testing, flooding or high-volume scanning.
- Don't try to trick our staff or members.
- Give us time to fix it before you tell anyone else.

The services we use for payments, email and video are theirs to test, not ours. Good-faith research that follows these rules is welcome.

More at [realadult.media/engineering](https://www.realadult.media/engineering#security).
