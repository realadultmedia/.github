## Communities for adults.

We curate communities for adults looking for their people, and somewhere worth staying once they find them.

**Our communities**

- [realkink.men](https://www.realkink.men): where men into kink stop pretending
- pup.space: where the pack runs free (coming soon)
- furrow.social: where every fursona finds its people (coming soon)

**Open source**

Small pieces we needed and built well enough to give away. Each one runs in production on our own sites first.

- [photo-metadata](https://github.com/realadultmedia/photo-metadata): strip location, camera and owner data from photos without re-encoding them (PHP)
- [expo-face-boxes](https://github.com/realadultmedia/expo-face-boxes): find faces on the phone, so they can be covered before a photo is shared (Expo)

How we build, and what we've learned along the way: [realadult.media/engineering](https://www.realadult.media/engineering)
